# ADR-NNNN: Title

- **Status:** proposed / accepted / superseded / deprecated
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decision owner:** user or explicitly named authority
- **Approval record:** link to explicit user approval, or state pending

## Context

Describe the decision, scope, and constraints. Separate observed facts from historical claims that were not re-run.

## Decision

State the durable decision and exact boundary it establishes.

## Consequences

Describe benefits, costs, migration impact, risks, and what the decision does not authorize.

## Alternatives

List realistic alternatives and why they were not chosen or remain open.

## Evidence

List commands, documents, tests, measurements, or approvals that support the decision. Mark unmeasured evidence clearly.

## Affected Contracts

List product specifications, architecture contracts, work-order boundaries, schemas, APIs, or operational procedures affected.

## Rollback or Supersession Conditions

State what evidence, approval, or replacement ADR is required to reverse or supersede the decision.
